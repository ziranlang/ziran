av1

Owned scalar AV1 decoder in handwritten Ziran. DecodeAv1YUV from av1/decoder decodes one shown KEY frame to source-depth YUV planes, including deblocking, CDEF, loop restoration and whole-frame film grain. Inter frames, intra block copy, superres, non-flat quantizer matrices and metadata OBUs reject with UnsupportedCapability.

In progress No tagged release yet.

Overview

It matches the pinned libaom decoder bit-exactly on the local test corpus and on 275 of the 279 KEY temporal units in libaom's pinned test vectors. That is not a full AV1 conformance claim. The scalar decoder is still several times slower than libaom and dav1d; tests/av1_benchmark.sh measures it. See docs/AV1.md for the module layout, contracts and tests.

sh tests/av1_critical_path.sh

libaom and dav1d are pinned test oracles only; no product module calls a native codec. tests/common.sh and tests/av1_headers.sh build them under ignored build/native when missing. Ported libaom constants keep their notices in src/LICENSE.libaom and src/PATENTS.libaom.

Owned source-domain samples, independent of the encoded input. Monochrome returns Y alone; color returns independent Y/U/V planes of the source depth.

Av1StillWorkingBytes :: 1048576

Authored scalar subset, not a full AV1 engine: 8/10/12-bit mono/420/422/444 shown KEY frames with intra tools, palette, CfL, filter intra, lossy and lossless transforms, segmentation, delta q/lf, multiple tiles, deblocking, CDEF, loop restoration and film grain. Superres, quantizer matrices and intra block copy reject explicitly; no bridge or native decoder is called. Do not switch the AVIF default until the full required frame/plane corpus passes.
