av1

Owned scalar AV1 decoder in handwritten Ziran. DecodeAv1YUV from av1/decoder decodes one shown KEY frame to source-depth YUV planes, including deblocking, CDEF, loop restoration and film grain up to 64x64. Inter frames, intra block copy, superres and non-flat quantizer matrices reject with UnsupportedCapability.

In progress No tagged release yet. The repository is not public yet.

ContentsOverviewdecoder

Overview

It matches the pinned libaom decoder bit-exactly on the local test corpus. That is not an AV1 conformance claim, and no speed has been measured. See docs/AV1.md for the module layout, contracts and tests.

build-queue -- sh tests/av1_critical_path.sh

libaom and dav1d are pinned test oracles only; no product module calls a native codec. tests/common.sh and tests/av1_headers.sh build them under ignored build/native when missing. Ported libaom constants keep their notices in src/LICENSE.libaom and src/PATENTS.libaom.
