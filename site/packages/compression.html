compression/src/inflate.zi

inflate

RFC 1951 DEFLATE and RFC 1952 gzip, implemented entirely in Ziran. Callers own a bounded output buffer. Malformed trees, distance overruns, truncated input, CRC mismatch and trailing streams are rejected.

Import with #import "compression/inflate".
