std/audio

Audio mixer: voices of decoded or streamed sound, each with its own gain, pitch and loop, mixed into interleaved float32 stereo at 48 kHz, with a tap that sees every mixed block. It owns no device: a sink (std/audio_sink) asks it for blocks from its audio thread, and a test can render into a buffer of its own. Decoders join through AudioUseDecoder (std/audio_decode_wav, std/audio_decode_mp3); each recognizes its content, never a file name. A sound loads decoded whole; music decodes while it plays. A PCM stream plays signed 16-bit blocks the program submits and never finishes: it plays silence until the next block. Every call here belongs to the application thread; while a sink runs, each holds the sink's lock, so the audio thread sees a voice either before or after a change.

Stable Shipped in release v0.7.0.
