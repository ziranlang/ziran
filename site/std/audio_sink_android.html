std/audio_sink_android

Android's output device for the shared audio mixer. The API-21 scaffold needs OpenSL ES on Android 5/6/7; devices with AAudio use float32 output. Public contract matches audio_sink_sdl. Call open/close on the application thread.

Stable Shipped in release v0.7.0.
