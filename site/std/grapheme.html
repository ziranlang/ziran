std/grapheme

Unicode 17.0 extended grapheme clusters (UAX #29, GB3–GB13). Byte offsets remain suitable for slices and wire protocols. A cluster is a user-perceived character; this module needs no font, display or host.

Stable Shipped in release v0.4.2.
