std/guest_host

Native, non-window capabilities shared by portable-program hosts. One State belongs to each hosted instance. It borrows its bundle and argument vector; returned text borrows answer storage until the next host call, while asset bytes remain borrowed from the open bundle.

In progress Available on master (Unreleased).
