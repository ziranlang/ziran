std/guest_window

A window for a portable bundle. The bundle draws its own pixels and runs its own loop: open the window, take events, present a frame, wait. The program that runs the bundle, such as ZX Runner, shows the pixels and reports input. One window per bundle.

Stable Needs: Host capability. Shipped in release v0.9.0.
