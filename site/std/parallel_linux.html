std/parallel_linux

Reusable native range workers. One owner submits non-overlapping ranges and joins their effects before continuing. Keep the pool at one address until ParallelClose; callbacks must not submit to or close their own pool.

Stable Needs: Native Linux. Shipped in release v0.3.1.

Matches checked #parallel regions: four lanes by default, including the submitting thread. ZIRAN_PAR_THREADS=1 is useful for parity and profiling.

Half-open [start, end). The caller guarantees callbacks write only their own range and do not mutate inputs used by another lane. Returning is a completion barrier; all writes are visible and borrowed arguments reusable. Small ranges and thread-creation failure retain exactly the same behavior.
